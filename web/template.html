Reduce Noise cleans the clip at the amount set on the slider: 100% is the full effect and 0% leaves the audio untouched. After an upload, a noise check names the most likely kind of noise. Pick a version to switch while it plays; the position is kept, so you hear the same moment each way. Click a waveform or spectrogram to jump. Waveforms share one vertical scale, so you can see how much each version removes. Audio you upload is processed on this device as mono at 16 kHz, first 20 seconds only, and never leaves it.

How it works

DSP in

Analyse and track the noise

A 512-point STFT feeds 40 mel bands. A running low percentile of each bin follows the noise floor, with no need to capture a noise print.

Neural

Predict 40 band gains

A small network reads the band energies plus the DSP stage's own signal-to-noise estimate, using past frames only, and outputs one gain per band.

DSP out

Smooth and resynthesise

Attack and release smoothing and a gain floor tame artefacts. Gains are spread back across FFT bins and the audio is rebuilt by overlap-add.

The spectral gate uses the same analysis and smoothing, with a Wiener-style gain rule where the hybrid has its network. That makes the comparison about one thing: who decides the gain.

  • Training and test audio is synthesised: a pulse source through formant filters stands in for speech. Real dialogue will be harder.
  • The spectral gate has not been carefully tuned, so the gap likely flatters the hybrid.
  • On babble, the one noise type the network never saw, the gain is small. Expect the same on real recordings until it is trained on real data.

Next: train on real speech and room noise, add PESQ and STOI scores and a listening test, extend to de-reverb, then port to a real-time JUCE plugin.
